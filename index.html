STEP 05

Recovery suggestions

Possible strategies when processes remain unfinished.

Run detection first

Recovery guidance will be based on the unfinished processes reported by the C++ engine.

01

Process termination

Terminate one process involved in the deadlock to release its allocated resources. Process priority and lost work should be considered.

02

Resource preemption

Take a resource from a selected process where safe and supported, then reassign it to help another process proceed.

03

Rollback and retry

Restore a process to a previous checkpoint and restart it later. This requires checkpoint support.

These are advisory strategies. The simulator does not modify actual operating-system processes.
